# 2. Current situation and problem


### My starting point

I am personally very interested in finances — more than the average person. I enjoy building budgets and optimising my spending. So the problem is not that finances bore me. The problem is what the existing tools ask of me.

### What is on the market

I have tried a number of finance and budgeting apps. Most of them are genuinely good products. My issue with them is not quality, it is quantity: they offer too much. They invite you to lose yourself in details and planning, and most of them are built around tracking every expense day by day or maintaining a budget continuously.

Even as someone who likes this topic, I never felt like keeping that up. And if I don't, I doubt that most people ever will. An app that only works if the user is disciplined every single day is an app that most people will stop using after a few weeks.

I am not claiming that no simple app exists. I am saying I did not find one that fits what I was looking for: a tool that offers less, and that focuses only on what is real.

### What I do today

Because I was not happy with the apps I tested, I built an Excel sheet with exactly the few functions I need. It works well — well enough that half of my family now uses it too. That was the moment I realised this is not only my own need. At least in my personal environment, there is real demand for something this simple.

The weakness of the Excel sheet is accessibility. I don't always have my computer with me, and a spreadsheet is not something you quickly open on your phone while sitting at the kitchen table. A dedicated app also gives me a proper structure and safer handling of the data than a file that gets copied around.

### The gap

What I was missing is an app that asks for five minutes a month and gives an honest picture in return — one that looks backwards at real numbers instead of forwards at a planned budget.

### What this project is not

This product is not meant to reinvent the wheel, and it is not meant to be the best budgeting app ever built. It is meant to be a small, simple product that does one thing: keeping track of real numbers, once a month, with a minimum of effort.
