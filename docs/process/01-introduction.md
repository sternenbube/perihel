# 1. Introduction


### What this app is

A finance tracker that you only open once a month. You sit down for five minutes, write down the current balance of each of your accounts, and that's it. Over the months you can see whether your money is going up or down. Nothing else.

It does not build a budget, it does not import transactions, it does not categorise anything. It only records real numbers, as they actually are in your real accounts.

Most budgeting apps sell you a dream of a budget that was never going to work. This app gives you the truth instead of an illusion.

### Why I am building it

I want a finance app that gives me a good overview without asking much of me. The best finance app is one I don't have to use often and that still gives me a real benefit. I have not found one, so I am building it.

The second reason is learning. This project is not only about the app, it is about running a complete development process from the first idea to a finished product — analysis, decisions, design, implementation, testing and documentation. The app itself is deliberately small so that the process can be the actual focus. It is also my entry into mobile development.

### Who it is for

For people who want to get it done, get an overview, and spend as little time as possible on it. That is not only people who dislike the topic. I am very interested in finances myself, and even I was never willing to track every expense day by day — most people never will. This app asks so little that it works either way. Anyone who can spare five minutes a month can use it and will already learn something about their own situation.

The first version is built for myself and a small group of test users.

### Vision

This version covers what I consider the most important basics. Whether the app later grows into something bigger — with more functions on top of pure tracking — is left open on purpose. The goal for now is a finished, working product that does one thing well.
